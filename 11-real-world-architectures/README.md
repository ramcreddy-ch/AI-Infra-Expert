# Module 11: Real-World Architectures

Case studies on how top-tier technology companies build and manage their production AI platforms.

## 📚 Analysis and Case Studies
- **OpenAI:** Orchestrating tens of thousands of A100/H100 GPUs on Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) with custom scheduler configurations.
- **Meta:** Building the Research SuperCluster (RSC) and Grand Teton hardware platforms to run large-scale training pipelines.
- **Google:** Deploying TPU Pod fabrics and Jupiter network switches to train Gemini models.
- **Tesla:** Designing Dojo custom ASICs and cluster-level scheduling scripts.
